03 // How I run a call

One call, five stages

01
Open and set the frame: permission to ask questions, honest answers both ways
02
Discovery: who is moving, why now, what happens if it goes wrong
03
Present the outcome they described, in their words
04
Surface every question before answering any
05
Handle in order: uncertainty, spouse, money. Then close.

This is what I consider a good sales process. I'd like to learn yours and run your script.
