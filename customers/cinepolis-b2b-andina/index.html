03 // Business Lines
Four B2B Pillars

Four Revenue Pillars

A. Events

Screenings & Corporate Functions

  • School bulk bookings & group screenings (El Bibliotecom)
  • Corporate events, avant-premieres, private screenings
  • Birthday celebrations (Junior Room, expansion opportunity)
  • Room rentals for corporate presentations (off-peak hours)
B. Vouchers

Corporate Ticket Sales

  • ~60-70% digital codes, ~30% physical (migrating to all-digital)
  • All formats + candy bar combos
  • Third-party loyalty programs & HR departments
  • E-commerce platform: 10-400 tickets online
C. Partnerships

Commercial Alliances (largest revenue)

  • Banks, telcos, compensation funds offer 40-50% discounts
  • Fee model: 20-40% of ATP per ticket or fixed annual fee
  • Partners invest in advertising + events + corporate tickets
  • 11% of 2025 admissions came from partnerships
D. Media / Advertising

Screen & Lobby Advertising

  • 3 tiers: Pre-showtime (cheapest) → Traditional slot → Premium (2 min before film)
  • Lobby: digital screens, standees, floor graphics, bathroom
  • Sold through FlixMedia + direct for global deals
  • NEW: local cinema advertising to nearby businesses

Vouchers
Vouchers

Best Practices

B2B E-Commerce Platform

  • Small purchases (under 400 tickets) increased by 100%
  • tickets.cinepolismedia.cl: 10-400 tickets purchasable online
  • Automated code delivery, no manual intervention
  • Enabled participation in Cyber Monday, Black Friday
  • Closed employee-benefit campaigns (company communicates, employees buy at special prices)

Key Client Management

  • Ongoing contact with key corporate customers
  • Collaborating with B2B teams of partner companies
  • Growth through public tenders and government sales
  • Per-cinema sales channels with tracked fulfillment (2026)

360° Communication

  • Paid campaigns during key retail dates (Mother's Day, Children's Day, Labor Day)
  • Ongoing communication: Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, website
  • CRM segmented emails by movie releases and key dates
Opportunities to Grow Further
  • Other Income Program: cinema managers as local sales agents with targets and incentives
  • Voucher Management API: real-time redemption control as partner asset
  • AI-assisted leads: real-time feedback to prospects
  • Scale Chile digital sales automations to Peru and Colombia

04 // Best Practices
Partnerships
Partnerships

Best Practices

Partners subsidize discounts (40-50% off) and pay fees ranging from 20-40% of ATP. Two models: per-ticket fee or fixed annual fee.

Telefonica
